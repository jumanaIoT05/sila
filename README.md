# Sila | صِلة 💵

**Sila is an AI-powered personal finance app that helps users understand, organize, and manage their finances in one place.**

Instead of relying on direct bank integrations, Sila uses bank SMS messages to record financial transactions and provide a unified view of accounts, spending, budgets, savings goals, and financial health.

## ✨ Features

* **Unified Financial Dashboard:** View account balances, income, expenses, and an overview of your financial activity in one place.
* **SMS-Based Transaction Tracking:** Parse bank SMS messages to record transactions without direct bank integration.
* **Automatic Transaction Categorization:** Organize transactions by type and spending category.
* **Budget Management:** Set spending budgets and monitor your progress.
* **Savings Goals:** Create financial goals and track your progress toward achieving them.
* **Financial Health Score:** Get an overview of your financial habits and progress.
* **AI-Powered Insights:** Receive personalized insights and recommendations based on your financial data.
* **Spending Analysis:** Explore your financial activity through summaries and visualizations.
* **Account Management:** Keep track of multiple financial accounts in one dashboard.

## 🧭 How to Use Sila

### Try Sila Online

You can explore Sila directly through the live application:

[**Open Sila**](https://sila-six.vercel.app/)

To explore the app using sample data:

1. Open the application using the link above.
2. Click your **profile picture** to open the profile menu.
3. Navigate to **Settings**.
4. Select **Demo Mode** to explore Sila's features using demo data.

### Explore the Features

1. **Review your dashboard:** Explore account balances, financial activity, budgets, goals, and your financial health score.
2. **Track transactions:** Open the Activity section to review transactions and add new ones using supported bank SMS messages.
3. **Manage your budget:** Review your spending and track it against your budget.
4. **Track your goals:** Create savings goals and monitor your progress.
5. **Explore AI insights:** Review available insights and recommendations to better understand your financial habits.
6. **Manage your accounts:** View and organize your financial accounts in one place.

Sila aims to make personal finance management more accessible by bringing financial information together, helping users understand their spending habits, and supporting more informed financial decisions.
